\section{Declaration of AI Assistance}
\label{appendix:ai_assistance}

The authors used ChatGPT and Google Gemini as assistive tools during preparation of the seminar materials. The assistance included:

\begin{itemize}
    \item suggesting an initial structure and section order for the Visitor seminar report;
    \item reviewing draft text, rewriting selected passages in formal academic English, and generating or refining LaTeX formatting from author-provided outlines and content;
    \item suggesting considerations for a C++ micro-benchmark, including avoiding console I/O inside the timed region and retaining an observable result to reduce the risk of dead-code elimination;
\end{itemize}

The benchmark methodology was informed by AI suggestions. The authors adapted those suggestions, implemented the submitted C++ programs, executed the measurements, and are responsible for reporting the environment, procedure, limitations, and results. AI-assisted text and visuals were reviewed and revised by the authors before inclusion. The authors retain responsibility for source selection, citations, code behavior, interpretations, originality, and the technical accuracy of the final submission.
